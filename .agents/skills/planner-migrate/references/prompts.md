# Migration prompts

Use these as request starters. Substitute only confirmed roots, scopes, and
identities; discover native tool schemas before proposing calls.

## Inventory and map

> Inventory `[source root]` read-only for Planner migration into `[project/root]`.
> Verify consumer tool readiness and selected canonical root separately from
> migration coverage. Classify every source, read the full candidate content,
> hash it, trace links/ownership, and return a dry-run source-to-target map with
> ordered queue, unresolved fields, dispositions, and coverage totals. Do not write,
> contact YouTrack, or alter legacy sources.

## Approved representative batch

> Execute only the approved representative `[whole sprint / linked cluster]` from
> `[approved map]`. Recheck source hashes, use native Planner writes and readback,
> resolve forward links second, and maintain the approved ledger. Preserve source
> evidence and authority; stop on conflicts or unsupported preservation. Report tool
> readiness separately from this batch's coverage. Do not begin another batch.

## Resume and completion audit

> Resume `[batch]` using `[ledger]`: verify topology, native tool readiness, and each
> pending source hash before any write. Reconcile every inventory item to one
> disposition, then audit source/target substance, links, doctor results, and
> active/dependency behavior. Report unresolved blockers and coverage separately;
> do not claim migration complete merely because tools are ready or doctor is green.

## Historical packet repair

> Repair the approved existing historical packet identity from `[source]`; do not
> recreate it. First build and review a full source-section-to-target-artifact map
> (review evidence may be in BRIEF or PROGRESS), including conflicting source
> statuses and QA marked n/a. Neither establishes Done or a new PASS. Discover the
> installed schema, then enrich through supported `historicalContent` or
> `planner_historical_enrich` when available, with required revision/hash checks.
> Read back the complete result, preserve provenance, and report any unsupported
> content as a blocker rather than truncating it.
